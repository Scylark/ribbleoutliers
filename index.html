CP 01 · THE DIRECTION ››

The sport has picked a direction

Professional gravel is mid-way through a structural shift. The privateer model that defined the field three years ago is breaking, and team-based racing is taking its place.

The clearest signal came at Unbound Gravel 200. Keegan Swenson and Mads Würtz Schmidt broke clear for Specialized Off-Road, Schmidt punctured with 75 miles to go, and Swenson handed over his own wheel so his teammate could ride on to the win.

The startlists tell the same story. Rosa Klöser went from winning Unbound to a WorldTour contract, and Ben Swift lined up at the British Gravel Nationals in August.
